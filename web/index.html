Conway's Game of Life

Original idea by John Horton Conway

A zero-player game: you plant the first cells, and four rules decide everything that follows.

  • A live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours survives.
  • A dead cell with exactly 3 neighbours comes to life.
  • Anything else dies of loneliness or overcrowding.

From those rules alone come gliders, oscillators and colonies that look uncannily alive.

Drag to draw life — even while it is running.
The board wraps at the edges, so a glider that leaves one side comes back in on the other.
